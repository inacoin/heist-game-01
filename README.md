# Midnight Fox Heist

A top-down stealth heist game for the make.fun "Make the best heist game" bounty. Slip through three museum rooms, steal the Star Diamond in each, and reach the exit before the alarm hits 100%.

- Guards patrol with vision cones (amber) and chase when they spot you; cameras sweep red cones.
- The alarm meter rises while you are seen and falls while you hide. At 100% the museum locks down for 20 seconds and the guards hunt.
- Grab gold bars, paintings, and jewels for extra loot; the diamond is required to open the exit. Three lives, score and time bonuses for a clean, quiet run.
- Keyboard (WASD/arrows to move, Shift to sneak, E to grab, P/Esc to pause) and mouse. No network, no build step.

Play it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
